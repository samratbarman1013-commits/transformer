Once upon a time there was a little robot named Pip who lived in a small blue house.
Pip loved to count the stars every night before going to sleep.
One night Pip counted ten stars, and the next night he counted twelve stars.
His friend Lily the firefly came to visit and they played hide and seek in the garden.
Lily hid behind a big red flower and Pip could not find her anywhere.
Then Lily laughed so loudly that Pip heard her and found her at last.
They both laughed and promised to play again tomorrow.
In the morning Pip ate a breakfast of nuts and berries by the river.
The river was wide and slow, and little fish jumped out of the water to say hello.
Pip waved at the fish and the fish waved their fins back at him.
After breakfast Pip went to the workshop to build a small wooden boat.
He measured the wood carefully with his ruler and sawed each piece to the right size.
The boat took three days to finish, and on the fourth day he painted it blue.
When the paint was dry, Pip carried the boat down to the river.
The little boat floated on the water and did not sink, and Pip was very happy.
All of his friends came to watch the boat sail across the quiet river.
The sun was warm and the sky was clear and everyone smiled.
When the evening came, Pip went home and told his mother about his wonderful day.
His mother hugged him and said she was proud of her little robot.
Pip ate his dinner, brushed his teeth, and got into his cozy bed.
Before closing his eyes he counted the stars one more time.
There were ten stars, then eleven, and then he fell fast asleep.
The next morning Pip woke up early because he heard a strange noise outside.
It was a small brown bird singing on his window.
The bird sang a happy song, and Pip hummed along with the tune.
Pip and the bird became good friends, and every morning she sang for him.
Every evening Pip told the bird a story about his day.
And so the days went by, warm and slow and full of small joys.
